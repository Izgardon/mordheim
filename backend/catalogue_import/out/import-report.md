# Catalogue import report, first pass

mordheimer's items plus our MiM list, cleaned into `out/`. Generated by `build.py`; do not edit by hand.
51 shared rules, 43 of them worded differently on some items (those items keep their wording as a text override).

## Start here

1. **Same-name list:** 8 names appear more than once across mordheimer and MiM. Mark each main or other (below).
2. **Warband groups:** 19 membership questions. The big ones: whether chaos humans count as Humans, and that Good, Evil and Mercenaries hold only the 75 warbands mordheimer has (they come from its hiring lists).
3. **Items no one may buy:** 14 mordheimer items exclude every warband and permit none, mostly Khemri's. Defaults are in the contradictions table.
4. **mordheimer's contradictions:** 36 places where an item's lists and its own notes disagree, each with the default taken.
5. **Warband names:** 15 names in mordheimer's data match no warband type, or more than one. "Chaos Dwarfs" is mapped to Black Dwarfs and Wolfcloak's "Mercenaries" is left out; please check.

Answers go into `overrides.json`; the next build applies them.

## Warband group questions

Good, Evil and Mercenaries come from mordheimer's hiring lists, so the 88 Broheim-only warband types are in no side group yet. Fill them in later, or leave them?

**Humans** (78 types)
- Chaos humans (the Possessed, Norse, Marauders, Carnival of Chaos) are in Humans as well as Chaos. Keep them?
- Not in Humans, though some of the 6 mordheimer lists that mean it (Big Game Hunter, Imperial Tactician, Heinrich 'Altdorf' Schmidt, Riding/Draft Horse, ...) name them: Undead (3/6), Mootlanders (2/6), Pit Fighters (6/6), Black Dwarfs (3/6). Add any?

**Goblins** (11 types)
