Bestiary and hirelings

Split today's bestiary into what it really holds. Hired swords and dramatis personae get their own catalogue tables and copy into one warband unit kind, the hireling. The bestiary keeps three kinds of beast. Every beast is an item first: a mount or pet is switched on for the unit carrying it, and a creature turns into henchmen.

Plan only, nothing built 22 Oct 2026 Built from our current data Fresh migrations and seeders

In short

  • Hirelings. The warband's hired_sword unit and its tables are renamed hireling. A hireling is a hired sword or a dramatis persona; it holds every field itself and points at nothing.
  • Two catalogue tables. hired_sword and dramatis_persona stand alone, with no bestiary link, each with its own skills, specials, spells, items and restrictions. Hiring copies one onto the warband, minus its fluff, on the server.
  • Three kinds of beast. A companion belongs to one unit and is either a mount, which changes the rider's shown M and armour save, or a pet, with or without stats. A creature becomes henchmen or joins a group of the same beast.
  • Every beast has its item. "Available in the shop" only decides whether the item is sold; ticking it in the form reveals the price fields.
  • One button, on the unit that holds the item. A mount or pet in a unit's items is switched on for that unit and back off. A creature, in a unit's items or the stash, is recruited into henchmen and can go back to an item. Buying offers both straight away.
  • Rebuilt, not patched. The old profile link, the client-side hire copy and the catalogue's hired-sword special cases go. Migrations restart from a fresh 0001 and the seeders are rebuilt from today's data files.

Decided since version 2

  • Every beast always has its Animal item. The shop checkbox lists it or hides it; it never removes it.
  • A mount or pet is a state of the item row it already sits in, not a new table. It can only be switched on for the unit that holds the item; to give it to someone else, move the item first.
  • "Companion" is the joint term; its two kinds are Mount and Pet.
  • Buying a mount or pet offers "assign straight away", and buying a creature offers "recruit straight away".
  • The smaller points: recruiting from an item never charges again; henchmen groups can ride, one mount per member; the rename ships alone first; migrations reset now, while there are no real users.

Decided in version 4

  • Names: "hireling" in the code only. The site says "Hired sword" and "Dramatis persona".
  • A mount changes Move and armour save, for display only: the rider's M and save are worked out from the mount and highlighted on the warband page and in battle. The unit's own stats in the database never change.
  • Warnings, never blocks, for two mounts on one unit and for more than two mounts in a Mordheim warband.
  • When a unit dies, its companions follow its items.
  • Mount survival is a suggested roll in postbattle when the rider was taken out of action.
  • Mutiny in Marienburg beasts are seeded now; the duplicate Wardog is dropped.
  • Pets with a profile can take part in a battle as their own model (step 5).

Hiring

Hiring moves to the server: POST hirelings/hire/ with the catalogue row's id. "Hireling" is the code's name only; the site keeps saying "Hired sword" and "Dramatis persona". The server copies the profile minus its description, with item quantities and both cost formulas, records the HIRE ledger row and answers the new hireling. That removes the browser copy that drops the Ogre's second sword and every formula today.

  • One "Hire" button with two lists: hired swords and dramatis personae (empty until they are scraped).
  • Mounts in a kit: the Freelancer, Highwayman, Roadwarden and Hobgoblin Scout come with an Animal item, switched on as their mount on hire and included in the fee.
  • Dramatis personae copy with no XP, the Hero injury table, how long they stay and what they're paid in. After a battle, one who stays for one battle is suggested to leave.
  • Upkeep is suggested after each battle as today. A formula that isn't gold ("1 piece of wyrdstone") shows as a reminder rather than a charge.
  • The cap stays on hired swords (max 3). Dramatis personae are one of each, with a warning, never a block.

Questions for you

  1. Dramatis personae and the hired sword cap. Under the rules a dramatis persona is a unique character found after a battle, often for one battle only, who never gains XP and uses the Hero injury table. Hired swords have no numeric limit in the rules beyond one of each type; the max of 3 is our campaign setting. So: should a dramatis persona take one of those 3 slots, or sit outside them (one of each, warned)? A third option is a separate campaign setting for dramatis personae.Suggested: outside the cap, one of each, warned
  2. A "dismount" toggle in battle that puts the rider's foot M and save back for the rest of the battle, if he gets off his horse.Optional: in step 5 or later
